# LinkedIn post

**We turned a live GA4 dataset into a client-ready, on-brand report — in one sitting.**

The challenge: analytics data is everywhere, but a *story* a client can act on is rare. Pulling 90 days of channel data, comparing it year-over-year, designing it to brand, and writing the narrative usually takes days and three tools.

So we tried something different.

**The approach:**
→ **Coupler.io** pipes MIDE's GA4 straight into the workflow — no exports, no copy-paste.
→ **Claude Code** queries it live, applies the **LIN3S design system** (type, colour, voice), and hand-builds a 6-page interactive infographic — data, charts and story in one self-contained web page.
→ A human framed the questions, set the brand, and checked every number.

**The result** wasn't just pretty — it found something. MIDE nearly **doubled its audience** (+92.8% users) in 90 days… yet **revenue slipped 5.9%**. The paid-acquisition surge bought reach, not relationships — and Organic Search revenue quietly fell **74%** while its traffic barely moved.

That's the insight a dashboard hides and a story surfaces.

Data that's measured gets managed. Data that's *told* gets acted on.

**Powered by data. Driven by humans.**
