03Where the growth came from

Winners, losers,
and watch-outs

Paid channels drove almost all the extra traffic. Sorted by year-over-year change in users — the bars tell you who grew; the bubbles tell you whether it was worth it.

Winners

Paid Search scaled the right way — users +144%, revenue +184%, the best conversion rate on the board at 9.15%. Direct grew +45% and stayed the revenue backbone.

Watch-outs

Paid Other and Paid Social added ~8,850 users between them and returned about €100 combined. Reach was bought; revenue wasn't.

06What to do about it

Four moves for
the next 90 days

The audience is here. The job now is to turn reach into revenue — starting where the gap is widest.

01

Rebuild Organic Search value

Traffic and engagement held; monetisation didn't. Chase the ~€25.6k gap first — audit conversion tracking, then high-intent queries.

Biggest single revenue loss of the year.
02

Put paid on a quality diet

Paid Other & Paid Social brought ~8,850 users, ~€100, and 67–79% bounce. Re-target for intent, not impressions.

Most of the new volume; least of the value.
03

Feed Paid Search

The efficient engine: +184% revenue, 9.15% conversion — best on the board. It has room to scale profitably.

Proven return; give it more budget.
04

Protect Direct

€62.7k and 53% of all revenue, up 26%. It's the loyalty base the paid surge is meant to feed — measure whether it does.

The backbone. Don't take it for granted.
The takeaway

Growth in volume isn't growth in value — until you make it so.
